Chicken Noodle

by coocking this you'll enjoy a protein + gluten food with spices that bring you tender feelings :D

Ingredients

  • you'll need a 500g marrinated chicken leg for each person you cooking for
  • a packet of noodle for each person
  • tea spoon of turmeric for two people
  • tea spoon of black pepper for each person
  • entire spoon of soy souce for each person

Instructions

  1. put the marrinated chicken on pan in oil pan with pans head on it, with low temp so it may take a 20 min to be roasted
  2. boil the water and add the noodle packets in it
  3. when you added the noodles season it with the named seasonings
  4. at the end of process add soy souce so it may tase fresh and salty
  5. add and mix the fried chicken to your noodle
